Who we are

U Hirable is a global hiring hub that connects ambitious companies with exceptional talent. It is run by a Global Talent Acquisition Partner based in Egypt, with hands-on experience recruiting for companies across the US, Europe and the Middle East.

We believe the right job changes more than a career. It can change the life of an entire family. That's why we treat every search with care, and every candidate and client as a long-term partner.
