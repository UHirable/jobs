Why these templates pass the ATS

Most companies run every CV through an applicant tracking system before a person reads it. Tables, two-column layouts, icons, photos and text boxes often get scrambled or dropped. These templates use one column, standard fonts (Calibri), real bullet points and plain headings, so the ATS reads your CV exactly as you wrote it.

They are free, have no U Hirable branding except one small line you can delete, and open in Microsoft Word.
