              How the CV match checker works

              Most companies run your CV through an ATS (applicant tracking system) that looks for the words in the job ad before a recruiter ever reads it. This tool does the same thing, instantly and for free: it pulls the skills, tools and key terms from the job description, checks which ones appear in your CV, compares your years of experience with what the ad asks for, and gives you one score plus the exact keywords to add.

              Your CV is read by your browser only. It is never uploaded to U Hirable or anyone else. If the ad is on another site, copy the description text and paste it here (sites like LinkedIn don't allow tools to read their pages directly).
