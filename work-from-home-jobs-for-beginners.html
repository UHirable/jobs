Work from home jobs for beginners & freshers

The online jobs that actually hire with no experience, where to find them, and how to apply for free. Written by a recruiter.

Can you really work from home with no experience?

Yes, but only in certain roles. Companies don't hire beginners for remote engineering or marketing leadership; they hire beginners where the work can be taught in a week and measured every day: customer support, data entry, moderation, transcription, virtual assistance and sales outreach. Start in one of those, do it well for 6–12 months, and the next remote job comes much easier.

Everything on this page is free to use. We never ask for payment, and neither should any employer: if a "job" asks you to pay for training, software or a background check, it is a scam.

1. Entry-level jobs you can do from home

🎧

Remote customer support

Answer customer emails, chats or calls for a company. The biggest source of beginner remote jobs worldwide. Needs good English, a quiet room and a reliable connection.

Typical pay: $3–$8/hr (outsourcing) · $15–$22/hr (US companies)
⌨️

Data entry & data labeling

Type, clean or label data, including AI training data (tagging images, rating chatbot answers). Repetitive, but flexible and easy to start.

Typical pay: $2–$10/hr, often per task
🗂️

Virtual assistant

Handle email, scheduling, research and admin for a founder or small business. Organised people with good written English do well here.

Typical pay: $4–$12/hr
🛡️

Content moderation

Review posts, images or videos against platform rules for social networks and marketplaces, usually through outsourcing companies.

Typical pay: $3–$10/hr, shifts
🎙️

Transcription & captioning

Turn audio into text. Pays per audio minute, so speed matters. A good first freelance job.

Typical pay: $0.30–$1 per audio minute
📞

Sales development (SDR)

Find and contact potential customers for a startup by email and LinkedIn. The best-paid beginner track, with a clear path to a real sales career.

Typical pay: $600–$1,500/mo + commission (global) · $45K–$65K (US)
📚

Online tutoring

Teach English or a school subject by video. Native or fluent English speakers can start quickly; some platforms need a degree.

Typical pay: $10–$25/hr
📱

Social media assistant

Schedule posts, reply to comments and make simple designs for a small brand. Build a tiny portfolio first with free tools like Canva.

Typical pay: $4–$12/hr

3. How to actually get hired

1

Make a one-page, ATS-friendly CV

No experience? Lead with skills and proof: your English level, typing speed, tools you know (Google Sheets, Canva, Zendesk), any volunteering or university projects. Build it free with our CV tools, then upload it to Lilo to see your closest open roles.

2

Apply in volume, but to the right jobs

Beginners need 50+ applications to get a few interviews. Tools like LoopCV and AIApply in Career Boost apply for you daily and are free to start. Keep the filters tight: "customer support", "entry level", "remote".

3

Prepare the three questions every beginner gets

"Tell me about yourself", "Why remote?", and "How do you handle an angry customer?" Write a 60-second answer to each and practise it out loud. Test your internet, camera and a quiet background before the call.

4

Spot the scams

Real employers never ask for a fee, never pay by cheque before you start, never interview only on Telegram or WhatsApp, and never ask for your bank login. If the pay sounds too good for a beginner role, it is.

Frequently asked questions

Which work from home job is easiest for a fresher?

Customer support and data entry. Both hire with no experience, train you, and only need good English and a stable connection.

Can I get a remote job with no experience and no degree?

Yes. Support, moderation, transcription, VA and SDR roles rarely require a degree. Online tutoring is the main exception.

Do I need to pay to get a work from home job?

Never. Any employer or "agent" asking for a fee, training payment or equipment deposit is a scam. All the sites and tools on this page are free to use.

How much can a beginner earn working from home?

Outsourcing roles pay roughly $300–$900 a month depending on your country; direct roles with US or European companies pay $15–$22 an hour for support and more for sales.
